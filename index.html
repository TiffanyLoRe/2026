Case study 01 / Keurig Dr Pepper

Keurig.com · FY15 · Customer Appreciation Sale

$11M
in 10 days

A personal, handwritten thank-you to Keurig’s customers, launched across email, landing pages and the website.

My roleProject Manager. Wrote the creative brief, then led and coordinated copywriting, creative development, legal approval and CMS execution.

Briefs · Copy · Legal · CMS · Digital production · Reporting

The result

$11MWeb sales in 10 days

Keurig.com, in the 10 days to March 3, 2015. Monday and Tuesday each topped $1M.

#1Coupon campaign of the period

THANKS25 drove 176K orders in 10 days, ahead of every other major P6 campaign.

+46%Sale-week revenue vs. last year

$7.6M against $5.2M in fiscal week 22. The goal in the brief was +10%.

27.5KCustomers new to file

Acquired through the THANKS25 code.

+27%Email revenue vs. last year

$9.3M for the period, 8% over target. The sale launched in email on Feb 23.

+23%Web orders vs. last year

Period close: 479K orders, with traffic up 17% and revenue up 16%.

The brief

Background
As part of the FY2015 P6 campaigns, Keurig.com offered a Customer Appreciation Sale: a percentage off for Loyalists (email and landing pages only) and for everyone else, Buyers and Prospects.
Goal
Meet, or ideally exceed by 10%, last year.
Audience
V1, Loyalists (email and landing pages only). V2, all others: Buyers and Prospects.
Offer
V1, 25% off sitewide + free shipping. V2, 20% off beverage packs + free shipping. Codes THANKS25 and THANKS20.
Concept
A personal, handwritten note. “A personal, sincere, authentic approach to celebrate and thank” customers.

How it ran

  1. 7 weeks outBrief

    Creative briefs go in with manager approval, as a general rule seven weeks before launch.

  2. Feb 9Assets due

    Web and email assets due, a minimum of two weeks before launch.

  3. Feb 23Launch

    Email goes out with 25% off + free shipping. Resend on Feb 26, last call on Mar 2.

  4. Mar 4Results

    “10 days, $11M.” Monday and Tuesday web sales above $1M, reported to the Digital Direct team.

The assets

24 deliverables across five channels, in two offer versions: V1, 25% off sitewide for Loyalists, and V2, 20% off beverage packs for everyone else. Every one shared the same idea: personal, sincere, authentic.
